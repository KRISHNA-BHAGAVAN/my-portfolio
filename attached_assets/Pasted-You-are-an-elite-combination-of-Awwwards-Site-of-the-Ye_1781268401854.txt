══════════════════════════════════════════
PHASE 1 — DESIGN AUDIT
══════════════════════════════════════════

Before making changes:

1. Explore the entire frontend/ directory.
2. Understand the current structure, components, assets, pages, animations, typography, and layout.
3. Identify every visual, UX, branding, interaction, accessibility, and storytelling weakness.
4. Determine what should be:
   - kept
   - redesigned
   - replaced
   - removed entirely

Do not become attached to the existing design.

If a complete redesign is better, perform a complete redesign.

Take inspiration from the quality level of:

- Apple product experiences

- Modern creative developer portfolios

Avoid:

══════════════════════════════════════════

- smooth interactions
- fast loading
- production-quality implementation
